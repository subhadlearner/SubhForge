## Operating rules

1. Exactly one incomplete checklist item is `ACTIVE` at a time.
2. Complete, review, persist, and close the active item before activating another.
3. Do not silently expand scope or design later items while the active item remains unresolved.
4. For every substantial mechanism, record:
   - which accepted requirement requires it;
   - which real failure it prevents;
   - the smallest sufficient implementation;
   - its effect on the December delivery constraint.
5. If a bounded item becomes H11-sized, stop and reconsider before investing further.
6. Test deterministic behavior deterministically. Keep live-model behavioral tests few and purposeful.
7. Real dogfood begins early and is part of qualification.
8. New scope after scope freeze requires an explicit trade: something in means something else out.
9. Repository artifacts are durable authority. Chat sessions are working discussions only.
10. A `DONE` status requires evidence recorded here or in a linked authoritative artifact.
